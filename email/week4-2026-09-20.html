🥇 Runner spotlight: Deena Kastor

Deena Kastor didn't hit her stride until her late 20s — she ran cross country and track at the University of Arkansas, then kept building patiently for years before it all came together. In 2004 she won the Olympic bronze medal in the marathon, and she went on to become the American record holder at the distance. Her secret was consistency: she believed in showing up and doing the small things right, day after day, long before the results showed up. Proof that big breakthroughs come from years of ordinary work.
