🥇 Runner spotlight: Steve Prefontaine

In the late '60s and early '70s, "Pre" ran cross country and track for Marshfield High and the University of Oregon, setting American records at every distance from 2,000m to 10,000m. He didn't just want to win — he wanted to find out who had the guts to go out hard and hold on. His fearless, front-running style still inspires distance runners today.
