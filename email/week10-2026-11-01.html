🥇 Runner spotlight: Eliud Kipchoge

Eliud Kipchoge is widely considered the greatest marathoner ever, but what he's most known for isn't just winning — it's how boring and consistent his routine is. Same easy pace on recovery days, same simple meals, same early bedtime, for years on end, long before anyone was watching. His favorite saying is "no human is limited," but what he really means is that limits mostly come from not doing the small things consistently enough. A great note to end the season on: the sharpen days, the easy days, the shakeouts — they all matter just as much as the hard ones.
