🥇 Runner spotlight: Mo Farah

Mo Farah moved to England at age 8 speaking almost no English, and running became the thing that helped him fit in and make friends. He stuck with it for years through near-misses and disappointing races before it all clicked: at the 2012 London Olympics, in front of his home crowd, he won gold in both the 5,000m and 10,000m — then did the exact same double again four years later in Rio. He's proof that patience and showing up, year after year, eventually pays off.
