🥇 Runner spotlight: Joan Benoit Samuelson

Joan Benoit Samuelson ran cross country and track growing up in Maine, and just weeks before the 1984 Olympic Trials she had knee surgery — most people thought her season was over. She raced anyway, won the Trials, then went on to win the very first women's Olympic marathon ever held, breaking away from the pack early and just never looking back. Nobody could catch her because she never gave them the chance. A good reminder heading into the biggest training week of the season: sometimes you just have to go for it.
