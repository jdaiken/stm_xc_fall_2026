🥇 Runner spotlight: Grete Waitz

Grete Waitz grew up running cross country in Norway and was already a world-class track runner before anyone thought she had a shot at the marathon. In 1978 she entered the New York City Marathon almost on a whim — and not only won, she smashed the world record by over two minutes, in only her second marathon ever. She went on to win New York nine times, more than anyone in history. She used to say the hardest part of a race is just believing you can finish it — everything after that is just running.
