🥇 Runner spotlight: Emil Zátopek

The Czech runner known as the "Locomotive" basically invented interval training as we know it — he'd run repeat 400s over and over, way more than anyone else dared, and it made him nearly unbeatable. At the 1952 Olympics he won gold in the 5,000m, the 10,000m, AND the marathon (a distance he'd never raced before!) all in the same Games, a feat no one has matched since. He ran like it hurt because it did — and he believed that was exactly the point. Fitting inspiration for a week where the ladders get their longest rep yet.
