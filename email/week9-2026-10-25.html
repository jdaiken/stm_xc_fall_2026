🥇 Runner spotlight: Shalane Flanagan

Shalane Flanagan ran cross country all through school and racked up medals for years before she finally won the one race everyone said an American woman couldn't win anymore: the New York City Marathon, in 2017, the first American woman to win it in 40 years. What made her famous wasn't just winning, though — it was how she trained: she loved bringing other runners up with her, pushing training partners to become champions themselves instead of keeping her secrets to herself. Proof that teammates make each other faster.
