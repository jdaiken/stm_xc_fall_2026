Coaching notes

  • Drills stay the same all season so the kids learn them: high knees, butt kicks, A-skips, side shuffles, karaoke, then 2 strides. About five minutes.
  • Stretching closes every practice: quads, hamstrings, calves, hip flexors, held 20 seconds each, sitting in a circle. This is also the time to hand out reminders about the next meet.
  • Hill repeats mean run up hard, walk all the way down. The walk is the rest; don't rush it. Use the same hill every week so runners can feel themselves improve.
  • Track ladders build up to the longest rep and come back down. Rest is a walk of the same distance just run. If they can't talk during the walk, the rep was too fast.
  • Meet-week Fridays swap the distance run for a short shakeout. Kids race far better on fresh legs and lose nothing in fitness.
  • Lake Fairfax and Fort Hunt are grass-and-hill courses, so the hill days in weeks 7 and 9 are doing real work. Run them on similar terrain when you can.
  • Moving a runner up or down a group is fine. Go by what a runner can finish comfortably, not by grade alone.
